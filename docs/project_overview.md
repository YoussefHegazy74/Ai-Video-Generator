# Project Overview

**Status:** placeholder. This document will describe the product once core
decisions are made.

The AI Educational Video Generator will convert a selected page range from a
book or PDF into a personalized, long-form educational video presented as a
digital whiteboard lesson with synchronized narration. No instructor face or
human avatar will be shown.
