# Scene Contract

**Status:** placeholder. The canonical scene schema will be defined during
implementation.

The scene contract will be the shared boundary between planning, whiteboard
direction, packaging, generation, validation, and assembly.

## Intended contents

- Required and optional scene fields.
- Narration, timing, visual, and whiteboard instruction sections.
- Provider capability requirements (duration, resolution, audio support).
- Continuity references to adjacent scenes.
- Validation invariants and versioning strategy.
- Serialization and compatibility rules.
